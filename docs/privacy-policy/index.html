Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 July 2026 · Website: https://gangacounty.com

This Privacy Policy explains how Ganga County (“we”, “us”) handles information collected through gangacounty.com and related enquiry forms.

1. Information we collect

  • Contact details you submit (name, email, phone, message) via enquiry forms.
  • Technical data such as browser type, device, and approximate location (via analytics, if enabled).
  • Communication records when you call or message our listed phone / WhatsApp numbers.

2. How we use information

  • To respond to plot / site-visit / brochure enquiries.
  • To share project updates you request.
  • To improve website performance and content.
  • To comply with legal or regulatory requirements.

3. Sharing

We may share enquiry data with authorised sales partners or service providers (for example form delivery providers) solely to process your request. We do not sell personal data.

4. Cookies & analytics

The site may use cookies or similar technologies for basic functionality and analytics. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

5. Data retention

Enquiry data is retained as long as needed for sales follow-up and legitimate business records, unless a longer period is required by law.

6. Your choices

To update or request deletion of your enquiry details, contact us via Contact or call +91-9650655124.

7. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date above will change when we do.
