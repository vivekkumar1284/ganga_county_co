Step-by-Step Guide to Selecting Your East-Facing Plot

Securing land in a gated community requires a systematic evaluation. Follow these expert recommendations when visiting the site:

  1. Verify Road Width: Ensure your chosen east-facing plot is situated along a wide 30-ft or 40-ft bitumen internal road for effortless vehicle turnaround.
  2. Check Entrance Alignment: Confirm that the main pedestrian and vehicular entrance of your proposed villa falls in the auspicious pada (zones) of the east.
  3. Inspect Utility Prototyping: Verify that underground water lines, electricity conduits, and storm drains are already provisioned at your plot boundary.
  4. Review Legal Clearances: Ensure complete title transparency by inspecting Khatauni land records and RERA approvals.
