Overview of Garhmukteshwar Market

The Garhmukteshwar–Brijghat belt has seen rapid development due to spiritual tourism, highway connectivity, and expanding industrial nodes like Gajraula. Projects here fall into two main categories: government-approved gated residential townships like Ganga County, and unorganized agricultural/farm plot layouts.
