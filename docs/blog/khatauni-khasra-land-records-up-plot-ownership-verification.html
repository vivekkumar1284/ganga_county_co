Understanding Uttar Pradesh Land Records Framework

Investing in residential or commercial land in Uttar Pradesh—whether near established hubs or upcoming growth corridors like the Garhmukteshwar Ganga Expressway zone—requires bulletproof legal due diligence. Revenue departments in UP maintain digitized land records to ensure complete transparency for property buyers. However, real estate investors frequently get confused by regional legal terminology such as Khatauni (खतौनी), Khasra (खसरा), Shajra (शजरा), and Dakhil Kharij (दाखिल खारिज).

Failure to accurately check revenue documents can expose buyers to illegal land sales, unauthorized agricultural conversions, or disputed co-ownership claims. Khatauni vs. Khasra: Decoding Key Land Terminology

To successfully verify plot ownership in Uttar Pradesh, you must understand the distinction between document types used by Revenue Officers (Lekhpals and Tehsildars):

Khasra Number (खसरा संख्या)

A Khasra is a specific survey plot number assigned by the government to a parcel of land in a village or revenue estate. It specifies exact physical dimensions, total plot area (in Hectares or Square Meters), soil classification, and whether the parcel has access to public roads or canals. Think of the Khasra number as the plot’s unique identity card.

Khatauni Extract (अधिकार अभिलेख)

A Khatauni is the legal register of land rights. It consolidates all Khasra parcels owned by an individual or family within a specific revenue village. It lists the legal titleholder’s full name, father’s name, exact ownership percentage, mortgage encumbrances, bank liabilities, or pending revenue court disputes.

While a Khasra tells you what and where the land is, the Khatauni tells you who legally owns it and whether it is free from legal liabilities. Before executing a sale deed for gated township plots in Garhmukteshwar, reviewing an up-to-date Khatauni is mandatory.

5-Step Process to Verify UP Plot Ownership Online

Follow this step-by-step verification process using official Uttar Pradesh government web portals:

1

Access the Official Bhulekh UP Portal

Navigate to upbhulekh.gov.in. Select the 'Khatauni Ansh Nirdharan' or 'Khatauni Ki Nakal Dekhein' menu option to initiate your title search.

2

Select District, Tehsil & Village

Choose the property’s administrative location. For instance, select District: Hapur → Tehsil: Garhmukteshwar → Village/Mouza: Shahpur Chaudhary (location of Ganga County) or relevant revenue village.

3

Search by Khasra, Khata, or Owner Name

Enter the plot's Khasra Number provided in your property brochure or search by the seller's legal name. The system will pull the active revenue record entry.

4

Inspect Column 7 & Encumbrance Remarks

Examine Column 7 (Tippani / Remarks) of the Khatauni extract. Ensure there are no active bank liens, government acquisitions, or court stay orders registered against the land.

5

Cross-Check Land Use Conversion (Section 80)

For residential township development, confirm that the land has non-agricultural status under Section 80 of the UP Revenue Code. Township developments like Ganga County master plan layout come with pre-verified approvals.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What is the difference between Khatauni and Khasra in UP land records?

Khasra specifies the unique plot or survey number of a specific parcel of land, detailing its area and boundaries. Khatauni is a legal register extract listing the official owner(s), their share in the property, and land usage classification.

How do I check UP land records online on Bhulekh UP?

Visit upbhulekh.gov.in, select your District (e.g., Hapur), Tehsil (e.g., Garhmukteshwar), and Village/Mouza. Enter either the Khasra number, Khata number, or owner name to view and download the official Khatauni extract.

What is Dakhil Kharij (Mutation) and why is it essential?

Dakhil Kharij is the process of updating municipal or revenue records to transfer property ownership from the seller to the buyer after a sale deed is registered. Without mutation, you cannot legally pay land revenue or sell the land under your name.
