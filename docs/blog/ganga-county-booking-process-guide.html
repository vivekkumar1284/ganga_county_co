The 7-Step Journey: From First Enquiry to Legal Registration

To make your purchasing experience seamless, we have streamlined the acquisition process into seven clear, manageable phases. Here is exactly what you can expect at every turn:

Step 01

Initial Enquiry & Brochure Download

Begin by exploring our digital resources. Review the official pricing page or download our project brochure. Submit your contact details for an immediate callback from our property consultants.

Step 02

Personalized Site Visit

Experience the township firsthand. We arrange complimentary cab pickups from Delhi-NCR or guide you directly to Garhmukteshwar. Inspect on-ground infrastructure, park areas, and road widths.

Step 03

Plot Selection via Master Plan

Examine the master plan layout. Choose your ideal plot size (ranging from 150 to 500 sq. yards), corner orientation, park-facing units, or main avenue locations.

Step 04

Application & Token Payment

Submit your signed booking application form along with KYC documents (PAN, Aadhaar, photographs) and secure your chosen plot with a standard token booking amount via cheque, net banking, or UPI.

Step 05

Allotment Letter Issuance

Upon realization of the token amount and verification of your application, Atomoney issues an official Allotment Letter detailing plot dimensions, boundaries, and total consideration value.

Step 06

Payment Plan Execution

Fulfill the payment milestones according to your chosen plan (Down Payment or Construction-Linked Plan). Our finance desk assists with bank loan approvals from leading financial institutions.

Step 07

Registry & Possession Handover

Complete stamp duty formalities at the sub-registrar office in Garhmukteshwar. Receive your legal sale deed and formal physical possession handover to begin building your dream villa.

Frequently Asked Questions on Booking

Here are answers to common questions asked by first-time land buyers at Ganga County:

What is the initial step to book a residential plot at Ganga County?

The process begins with an initial enquiry via phone or website, followed by a guided site visit to explore available plot sizes, master plans, and pricing options.

What documents are required for booking a plot in Garhmukteshwar?

Required documents include PAN card, Aadhaar card, passport-size photographs, address proof, and booking payment instruments (cheque, net banking, or UPI).

Are flexible payment plans available during the booking process?

Yes, Atomoney offers structured payment schedules, construction-linked plans, and assistance with bank financing for eligible buyers.

How long does the final property registration take after booking?

Once all financial milestones for the plot are completed and statutory clearance is verified, the sale deed registration is scheduled at the local sub-registrar office with full legal support.
