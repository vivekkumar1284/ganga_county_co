Starting price, “on request” cards & price revision

Marketing for Ganga County often cites starting at ₹20,000 / Sq.Yd. with a down payment plan narrative. Portal cards may show “on request” until a counsellor quotes a specific unit. The site also highlights a possible price revision coming soon — so delaying a serious enquiry can change your ticket size. Check https://gangacounty.com/pricing.html and the homepage notice on gangacounty.com.

Some third-party pages quote ticket ranges “from ~₹30 lakh onwards” depending on size — useful as a ballpark only. Your binding number is whatever sales confirms in writing after a site visit booking through the contact page.

Down payment plan, interest-free instalments & EMI / loan

Project communications (including Atomoney channels) sometimes mention flexible / interest-free installment style payment plans. Portal listings have also referenced loan facility conversations with banks such as Axis Bank and HDFC Bank. Bank sanction depends on your profile and bank policy — Ganga County’s sales desk can guide documentation, but loan approval is never automatic.

  • Ask for the latest payment plan PDF / schedule.
  • Clarify booking / EOI amount vs allotment (application fee pages sometimes quote ~₹21,000 EOI — not allotment).
  • If you need EMI, start bank discussion early while you shortlist on the master plan.

Land for sale vs villa plots — search intent tip

People type land for sale in Garhmukteshwar, plot for sale, and villas in Garhmukteshwar interchangeably. Ganga County primarily sells residential villa plots (you build later), not ready-to-move villas. “Ready to move” usually does not apply to bare plots — plan construction timelines separately after possession of the plot.

Freehold / title questions

Buyers often ask whether inventory is freehold. Do not rely on blog copy for title type — request current title / approval papers during due diligence with the Ganga County sales team.
