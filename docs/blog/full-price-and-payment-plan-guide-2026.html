Starting Rates per Sq.Yd.

Base rates for residential plots at Ganga County start around ₹20,000 per Sq.Yd. Prices vary based on plot placement within the township layout—such as corner plots, park-facing units, or plots on wider 60-foot main boulevard roads.

Payment Plan Options

Ganga County provides structured payment schedules designed for ease of ownership:

  • Down Payment Plan: Attractive discounts for upfront payment completion.
  • Flexi Installment Plan: Timed interest-free milestone installments during site development.
  • Bank Loan Options: Approved project status simplifies home/land loan acquisition from financial institutions.
