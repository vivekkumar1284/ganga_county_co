Why Legal Verification Matters in UP

In Uttar Pradesh, land ownership records are maintained under dual systems: the District Revenue Department (Bhulekh / Khatauni) and Urban Development Authorities (GDA / RERA / Master Plan Layouts). Ensuring both systems align prevents future litigation, unauthorized demarcation issues, and zoning penalties.

1. Vital Revenue Records: Khatauni, Khasra, and Mutation

The foundation of land due diligence in UP lies within the revenue office (Tehsil). Before making any financial commitment or booking amount payment, independently verify these mandatory documents:

1. RoR / Khatauni Record

The Khatauni lists the legal owner's name, share percentage, total land area, and any existing mortgages or government encumbrances. Always verify the latest RoR on the official UP Bhulekh portal.

2. Khasra Map (Sajra Map)

The Khasra specifies the exact physical land plot number, boundaries, soil type, and spatial orientation. Ensure the plot shown on-ground matches the specific Khasra number in official village maps.

3. Dakhil Kharij (Mutation Certificate)

Check if the seller's name is updated in revenue registers through Dakhil Kharij. Without mutation, a registered title deed alone does not fully prove revenue ownership transition in UP.

4. Encumbrance Certificate (EC)

Obtain an Encumbrance Certificate for at least 13 to 30 years from the Sub-Registrar Office (SRO) to ensure the property is free from court disputes, bank loans, or third-party claims.

2. Section 143 / Section 80 Conversion Status

One of the most frequent legal oversights when buying plots in Uttar Pradesh is ignoring land conversion laws. Under the UP Revenue Code, 2006 (formerly Section 143, now under Section 80):

  • Agricultural vs Non-Agricultural: Land recorded as Krishi Bhumi (agricultural) cannot be legally used for permanent residential housing without a formal Sub-Divisional Magistrate (SDM) declaration.
  • Loan Eligibility: Nationalized banks and major NBFCs will never approve a plot loan for un-converted agricultural land.
  • Demarcation Security: Non-agricultural plots inside approved master plan townships protect buyers from future government acquisition or municipal penalty notices.

4. Physical Site Visit Checklist: Demarcation & Water Table

Never purchase land solely based on legal paper reviews. Conduct a comprehensive physical site inspection using this checklist:

Boundary Pillars & Corner Plots

Ensure concrete boundary markers are fixed by revenue surveyors (Tashildar/Lekhpal) to confirm precise plot dimensions without encroaching on neighbors.

Groundwater Quality & Table

Check the water table level and TDS quality. Townships near Garh Ganga enjoy high-quality fresh groundwater, perfect for farmhouses and custom villas.

High Tension Lines & Nala Distance

Verify that the plot is not located under High-Tension (HT) power cables or adjacent to natural flood channels (Nalas), as local laws prohibit construction near them.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What legal documents are required before buying a plot in Uttar Pradesh?

Key legal documents include the latest Khatauni (revenue extract), Khasra map, Title Deed, 13-year Encumbrance Certificate (EC), Section 143/80 non-agricultural conversion order, and sanctioned layout plan from local planning authorities.

What is Section 143 (Section 80) land conversion in UP?

Section 143/80 is an official declaration issued by the Revenue Court/SDM confirming that agricultural land is legally converted for non-agricultural (residential or commercial) use. Without this order, residential construction is unauthorized.
