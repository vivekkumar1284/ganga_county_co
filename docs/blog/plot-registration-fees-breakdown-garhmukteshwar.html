Detailed Component-Wise Breakdown of Plot Registration Costs

When you purchase a residential plot at Ganga County Garhmukteshwar, the final cost of acquiring the registered title deed comprises several distinct statutory and administrative elements. Let's analyze each one:

7%

Stamp Duty Charges

Mandatory state tax levied by the Government of Uttar Pradesh based on the predetermined circle rate or agreement value of the land parcel.

1%

Property Registration Fee

Standard administrative fee paid to the sub-registrar office for legally documenting and updating government land revenue records.

Circle Rate Valuation

The minimum benchmark valuation set by the UP district administration for land in Garhmukteshwar, serving as the baseline for stamp duty calculation.

Legal & Vetting Fees

Professional documentation charges for drafting sale agreements, allotment deeds, title verification, and encumbrance certificate checks.
