1. Overview of Property Registration in Uttar Pradesh (2026)

When purchasing residential land in Uttar Pradesh, stamp duty and property registration charges constitute the legal foundation of ownership transferred through the **Department of Stamps and Registration (IGRS UP)**. Stamp duty is a mandatory state tax levied under the UP Stamp Act, serving as official legal proof of ownership in Indian courts.

For property buyers acquiring gated freehold plots at Ganga County in Shahpur Chaudhary, Garhmukteshwar (Hapur District), calculating these costs in advance ensures complete financial clarity. Whether you are buying a 150 Sq.Yd. plot for a weekend holiday home or a 500 Sq.Yd. villa land, property registration legalizes your title deed (*Sale Deed* / *Bainama*).

The project developer, Atomoney Ganga County, provides full legal documentation support, ensuring seamless verification at the Sub-Registrar Office (SRO) Garhmukteshwar.

5. Required Documents Checklist & FAQs

To avoid delays during plot registration at the Garhmukteshwar Sub-Registrar office, buyers must keep the following documents ready:

  • Identity & Address Proof: Aadhar Card, PAN Card, and passport-size photographs of Buyer, Seller, and 2 Witnesses.

  • Allotment Letter & Possession Certificate: Official allotment paperwork issued by Atomoney Ganga County.
  • No Objection Certificate (NOC): Project clearance and layout approvals for the township.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the stamp duty percentage for buying plots in Uttar Pradesh in 2026?

In Uttar Pradesh, male buyers pay a 7% stamp duty rate on property valuation. Female buyers receive a 1% rebate on valuations up to Rs 10 Lakhs, effectively paying 6%.

Can stamp duty be paid online for plots at Ganga County?

Yes, stamp duty in UP is paid through the official e-stamping system managed by Stock Holding Corporation of India Limited (SHCIL) and integrated with IGRS UP.
