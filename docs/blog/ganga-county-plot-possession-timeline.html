Understanding the Journey from EOI to Plot Possession

Securing a residential plot in a prime gated community is an exciting financial milestone. However, first-time land buyers often wonder about the sequence of events following their initial booking. At Ganga County, developed by Atomoney Ganga County in Garhmukteshwar, the possession and handover process is structured for complete transparency, legal clarity, and ease of mind.

Whether you plan to build an immediate weekend sanctuary, a retirement villa, or hold the land as a high-growth investment asset near the Ganga Expressway corridor, knowing your exact timeline helps you plan funds, architectural blueprints, and construction schedules effectively. Let us examine the complete lifecycle step by step.

Quick Milestone Overview

  • Stage 1 (Day 1 - 7): Expression of Interest (EOI), plot selection, and booking confirmation.
  • Stage 2 (Week 2 - 4): Down payment completion and execution of agreement to sell.
  • Stage 3 (Month 2 - 3): Final payment clearance and legal registry documentation.
  • Stage 4 (Handover): Physical site demarcation, boundary pillars, and possession certificate issuance.

The 4-Stage Plot Possession Timeline at Ganga County

To ensure absolute clarity for every investor, the progression from booking to physical handover is broken down into four predictable phases:

Phase 1: Booking Confirmation & EOI Submission

Upon visiting the site or reviewing our master plan layout, you select your preferred plot (ranging from 150 to 500 Sq. Yd.). An Expression of Interest (EOI) or booking amount is deposited, and you receive your official provisional allotment letter outlining plot dimensions, facing direction, and block number.

Phase 2: Payment Plan Execution & Verification

Depending on whether you choose a construction-linked plan or a down-payment structure outlined in the official price list, installments are cleared. Our legal team conducts full title verification and prepares the draft sale agreement with transparent documentation standards.

Phase 3: Sale Deed Execution & Legal Registry

Once 100% of the plot value and applicable stamp duty/registration charges are settled, the formal sale deed is registered at the sub-registrar's office. This transfers absolute freehold ownership rights legally into your name, providing total security.

Phase 4: Physical Handover & Demarcation

Following registry, our site engineering team escorts you to your exact plot. Official boundary marking pillars are installed, and a formal Possession Certificate is handed over. You are now officially cleared to start boundary wall construction or villa planning!

Essential Document Checklist for Smooth Possession

To ensure your possession and registry process concludes smoothly without administrative friction, keep the following documents prepared:

Financial Receipts

Original booking receipts, installment clearance certificates, bank transfer statements, and No Dues Certificate (NDC) issued by the accounts department.

Identity & KYC Proofs

Self-attested copies of PAN card, Aadhaar card, passport-size photographs, and updated residential address proofs for all co-applicants.

Allotment & Agreement Papers

Original provisional allotment letter, signed buyer agreement copy, and executed power of attorney (if applicable for outstation buyers).

Post-Registry Documents

Certified Sale Deed copy, mutation documents (Daakhil Kharij), possession handover certificate, and plot site demarcation map.
