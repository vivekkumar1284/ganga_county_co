3. Step-by-Step Blueprint to Resell Your Plot Before Possession

Executing a secure plot resale prior to taking formal physical possession involves a structured multi-phase protocol. Adhering to these steps ensures legal protection, clean financial transfers, and maximum net returns:

Step 1: Financial Ledger Audit

Verify that all due installment payments against your plot are fully cleared with the developer (Atomoney Ganga County). A clean ledger without arrears is mandatory before initiating transfer requests.

Step 2: Secure Developer NOC

Submit a formal transfer application to the township management. Obtain the official No Objection Certificate (NOC) and pay the designated administrative transfer fees.

Step 3: Tripartite Agreement

Execute a formal tripartite deed between the original allottee (you), the secondary buyer, and the developer, clearly defining payment schedules, premiums, and ownership transfer rights.
