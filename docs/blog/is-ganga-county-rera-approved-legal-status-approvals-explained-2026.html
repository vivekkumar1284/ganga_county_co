Step-by-Step Guide: How to Verify Legal Status Before Buying

For buyers looking for absolute peace of mind, follow this 5-step due diligence workflow when securing a plot in Ganga County or the broader Garhmukteshwar real estate market:

  1. Verify Ownership Titles (Khatauni): Inspect the revenue record (Bhulekh UP) to ensure the seller’s name is recorded with undisputed ownership rights.
  2. Check Legal Non-Encumbrance Certificate (NEC): Obtain an NEC for the past 12–30 years to confirm that the property is free from mortgages, court liens, or legal disputes.
  3. Review the Sanctioned Master Plan: Cross-check the township map on our Ganga County Master Plan Page to confirm park reservations, road widths, and plot demarcations.
  4. Inspect On-Site Infrastructure: Perform a physical site visit to verify that plot boundary pillars match official dimensions. Browse our Ganga County Gallery for actual ground images.
  5. Execute Sale Deed & Mutation: Complete registration at the local Sub-Registrar Office in Hapur district, followed by formal revenue mutation into your name.
