Deconstructing UP Stamp Duty Concessions for Plot Registrations

When purchasing land, stamp duty and registration charges constitute a significant portion of the transaction cost over and above the Basic Sale Price (BSP). In Uttar Pradesh, standard stamp duty for urban and developing land parcels generally hovers around 7%. However, to promote gender equality and encourage female asset ownership, the UP government grants special concessions when the property is registered in the name of a woman.

For instance, if a plot is purchased in the sole name of a female family member or if she is listed as the primary co-owner, a notable reduction of 1% is applied to the stamp duty calculation. When purchasing premium villa plots in Garhmukteshwar ranging from 150 to 500 Sq. Yd., this 1% saving amounts to tens of thousands of rupees—funds that can be directly reinvested into interior finishing or landscaping your dream home.
