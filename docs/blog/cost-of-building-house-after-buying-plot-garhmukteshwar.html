Detailed Cost Components of House Construction

When calculating the total budget for constructing a villa on your plot, expenses are generally distributed across structural work, finishing materials, and statutory fees:

Raw Material Costs (Structure)

Accounts for roughly 50-55% of the total budget. Includes high-grade TMT steel bars, OPC/PPC cement, coarse sand, red bricks or AAC blocks, and aggregate stones required for pillars, slabs, and walls.

Labor & Contractor Charges

Accounts for 25-30% of total expenditure. Covers skilled masons, barbenders, electricians, plumbers, and shuttering teams either on a square-foot turnkey contract or daily labor rates.

Finishing & Interiors

Accounts for 20-25% of expenditure. Includes vitrified/marble flooring, modular kitchen fittings, sanitary ware, electrical wiring, doors, windows, and exterior/interior paint coatings.

Approvals & Documentation

Nominal administrative fees for architectural blueprints, structural stability certificates, and local development authority compliance checks to ensure legal sanctity.
