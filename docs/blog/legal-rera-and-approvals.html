Government Approval & Township Status

Ganga County is developed as a planned, government-approved gated residential township in Garhmukteshwar (District Hapur, UP). This ensures the master layout adheres to urban planning guidelines for road widths, green belts, and commercial spaces. Learn more on our project overview page.

RERA & Compliance Verification

Under UP-RERA guidelines, structured residential developments must comply with project disclosure norms. Ganga County’s layout planning and phase delivery schedules are designed to align with statutory real estate standards, providing financial and legal security for homebuyers.

Land Title & Mutation Check

A key factor that makes Ganga County a safe investment is clear, unencumbered land title ownership. Buyers are provided with proper sale deed execution, title search assistance, and mutation support upon completing plot allocation.

Essential Buyer Legal Checklist

  1. Verify title deeds and land ownership history.
  2. Cross-check layout approval plans with the Ganga County Master Plan.
  3. Review the official allotment agreement and payment milestones.
  4. Confirm bank loan approvals from recognized lenders.
