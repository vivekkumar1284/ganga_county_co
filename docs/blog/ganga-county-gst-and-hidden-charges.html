  • GST Status on Land: Sale of vacant land or bare residential plots does not attract Goods and Services Tax (GST).
  • Stamp Duty in UP: Standard government stamp duty applies based on circle rates and agreement value.

Deconstructing the Total Cost of Plot Ownership

When evaluating the investment required for a plot at Ganga County, it is essential to look beyond the basic per-square-yard rate. Here is a detailed breakdown of every cost component involved in acquiring your dream property:

Basic Sale Price (BSP)

The primary cost calculated per square yard based on your chosen plot dimension (ranging from 150 to 500 Sq. Yd.) and its specific orientation within the master plan layout.

Stamp Duty & Registration

Statutory government fees payable to the Uttar Pradesh revenue department during property registration and title deed execution.

Development & Infrastructure

Charges covering internal 30/40-ft paved roads, underground electrical cabling, water supply networks, boundary walls, and security gates.

Preferential Location (PLC)

Applicable only for premium units such as corner plots, wide road-facing plots, or park-facing plots as specified in the booking guidelines.

Maintenance Corpus Deposit

An initial one-time or structured contribution towards upkeep of community parks, solar street lighting, 24/7 security guard services, and common area cleaning.

Legal & Documentation Fees

Nominal administrative and legal vetting charges associated with drafting allotment letters, agreement to sell, and final sale deed papers.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to common financial and tax inquiries regarding plot purchases at Ganga County:

Is GST applicable on the purchase of residential plots at Ganga County?

Under current Indian tax laws, sale of raw/vacant land or bare plots does not attract Goods and Services Tax (GST). However, if bundled with specific construction or composite development contracts, applicable rates depend on the exact service scope.

What is the stamp duty and registration fee for property registration in Uttar Pradesh?

In Uttar Pradesh, the standard stamp duty for property registration is typically around 7% of the property value (with minor concessions for female co-owners), plus a 1% registration fee.

Are there any hidden maintenance or development charges at Ganga County?

Ganga County maintains complete financial transparency with zero hidden costs. All development charges for internal roads, underground electricity, water lines, and security infrastructure are clearly outlined upfront.
