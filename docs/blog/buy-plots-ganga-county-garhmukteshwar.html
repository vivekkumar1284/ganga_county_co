What is Ganga County?

Ganga County is a gated residential township concept in Garhmukteshwar, Uttar Pradesh, positioned for buyers who want villa plots instead of ready apartments. The official project site at gangacounty.com presents it as a planned community near Garh Ganga with plot inventory commonly ranging from 150 to 500 Sq.Yd.

For a structured introduction to approvals, lifestyle positioning and investment narrative, start with the Ganga County plot project overview. That page is the best first stop before you dig into price cards or the layout map.

Why invest in Ganga County?

Buyers usually compare townships on four axes: approvals, location, livability and exit liquidity. Ganga County’s public messaging focuses on a government-approved gated format, proximity to spiritual landmarks like Brij Ghat / Garh Ganga, and connectivity toward NH-9 and the Ganga Expressway corridor.

  • End-use living: build a custom villa on your own plot timeline.
  • Investment holding: land in organised townships can be easier to resell than fragmented village parcels.
  • Lifestyle stack: clubhouse, parks, security and retail planning are part of the pitch — see Ganga County amenities.
  • Regional growth narrative: industrial and logistics interest around expressway nodes can support long-term demand in the wider belt.

None of this replaces due diligence. Always verify current documents, inventory and payment schedule with the team via gangacounty.com/contact.html.

Location & connectivity advantages

  • ~20 minutes from Ganga Expressway
  • ~20 minutes from Gajraula Industrial Area
