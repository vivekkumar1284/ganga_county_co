Key Factors to Evaluate Before Selecting Your Plot

Plot size shouldn't be judged solely by financial capacity. Consider these core operational and practical variables before signing your land contract:

Road Width & Frontage

Check the plot's front elevation width relative to the internal sector road. Plots with wider frontage (e.g., 35 ft+) offer grander curb appeal and easier garage access. See internal road layouts on the Ganga County master plan page.

Outdoor Greenery Expectations

If your weekend routine involves hosting outdoor barbecues, yoga sessions, or maintaining a fruit orchard, prioritize plot sizes above 300 Sq. Yd. so that building footprints do not encroach on green areas.
