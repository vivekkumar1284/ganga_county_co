Ganga County Site Visit Checklist — Coming from Delhi NCR / Noida

A brochure cannot replace standing on the plot. Use this checklist when you visit Ganga County in Garhmukteshwar after travelling via NH-9 / NH-24 from Delhi, Ghaziabad or Noida.

Before you leave home

  • Book via gangacounty.com/contact.html or call +91-9650655124.
  • Skim location distances (Garh Town, railway, Brij Ghat, Expressway).
  • Shortlist sizes on the price list (150–500 Sq.Yd.).
  • Watch site videos on the homepage and gallery.

On site — what to inspect

  1. Gate security process and approach road condition.
  2. Plot facing, neighbouring open space, and distance to parks (use master plan).
  3. Internal road width (project materials reference 30 / 40 / 60 ft).
  4. Noise, flood risk feel after rain, and power/water status questions for the counsellor.
  5. Travel time back toward Delhi-NCR at your usual hour — not only the best-case weekend drive.

Documents & questions to ask

  • Current approval / title papers (do not rely on marketing claims alone).
  • Latest payment plan, booking vs EOI amount, and any price-revision timeline.
  • Possession / registry process timeline for the specific plot.
  • Whether loan discussions with banks are facilitated (terms always depend on the bank).
